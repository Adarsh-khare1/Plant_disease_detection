From leaf image to useful insight.

01

Capture: Take a clear image of the leaf.

Focus on symptomatic areas under natural field daylight.

02

Check: PlantDx checks whether the image is suitable for analysis.

Evaluates image clarity, lighting, and leaf coverage.

03

Analyze: The system analyzes visual characteristics of the leaf.

Examines foliar patterns, lesion margins, and discoloration.

04

Understand: Review the result, confidence, symptoms, and guidance.

Clear diagnostic findings with practical next steps.

Why this result?

Symptoms
  • Visible marginal yellowing
  • Localized spotting
  • Intact midrib structure
Management

Monitor surrounding plants, inspect lower foliage, avoid overhead watering, and consult a local agricultural extension service.

Principles of agricultural diagnostic rigor.

Clear

Results should be understandable rather than technically overwhelming.

Designed for immediate comprehension in the field without confusing jargon.

Evidence-oriented

The system is designed around image analysis, measurable confidence, and documented information.

Grounded in observable symptoms and agricultural extension references.

Built to evolve

The diagnostic pipeline can improve as better preprocessing and machine-learning models are introduced.

Continuously refined as agricultural datasets grow.
